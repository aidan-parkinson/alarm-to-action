Research highlights:
\begin{itemize}
	\item A novel interpretation of Hobbes Leviathan returning a Commonwealth Cost of Carbon, a candidate universal carbon premium.
	\item A demonstrative calculation of historical Coommonwealth Costs of Carbon from the Hobbesian position.
	\item A novel quantitative Commonwealth Service Rating that may be used as a market indice to characterise local activities.
	\item A stable proposition for a new trade association to support the novel instruments, inspired by the works of Rawls and Hobbes.
\end{itemize}\\
