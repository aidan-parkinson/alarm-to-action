To the Editors,\\

I am writing to submit our manuscript titled "A Comprehensive Approach to Ecosystem Services Management: From Alarm to Action" for consideration for publication as a Perspective article in Climate Services. This submission responds to a call for papers from the 3rd International Conference on Environment and Sustainable Development, where I presented the paper.\\

The manuscript provides a timely, critical evaluation of basic theory towards discovery of a universal carbon premium. Specifically, this article argues that following the Hobbesian position allows one to estimate a "Commonwealth Cost of Carbon" using simple and elegant methods that do not require privelaged information or expert computation. Not only could this novel insight yield benefits for worldwide civic participation, but also allows a history of carbon premiums to be estimated with much higher certainty than competing views. By bridging the gap between climate science and practical application, our piece offers actionable advances for a new trade association called the "Commonwealth of Peoples" and introduces a new "Commonwealth Service Rating" instrument to inspire a new marketplace in service performance.\\

We believe this manuscript is an ideal fit for Climate Services because it directly aligns with the journal's mission to bridge science and practice. It specifically addresses your audiences interest in adapting institutions to the limitations of our common global ecosystem.\\
